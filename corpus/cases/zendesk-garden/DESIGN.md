## Adaptation rules

Suitable contexts: support tools and service operations, and workflow products balancing human context with queue efficiency.

Preserve: support-work calm and approachable utility.

Poor fit: immersive entertainment products where operational calm is not the goal.

## Failure modes

**Stop 1.** Using friendly surfaces to downplay urgency.

**Stop 2.** Breaking common control behavior in one support channel.

**Stop 3.** Letting customer context disappear inside queue density.
