## Adaptation rules

Suitable contexts: wearable health, navigation, and notification tasks, and companion experiences with brief repeat interactions.

Preserve: glanceable hierarchy and round-screen composition.

Poor fit: long-form content, complex configuration, or data entry.

## Failure modes

**Stop 1.** Shrinking a phone layout onto a watch.

**Stop 2.** Placing key text against curved clipping zones.

**Stop 3.** Requiring long attention or multi-step typing.
