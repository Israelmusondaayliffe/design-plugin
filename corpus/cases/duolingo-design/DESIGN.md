# Duolingo Design

## Visual thesis

Mascot and illustration behavior make progress feel social rather than abstract. Type, controls, and shapes share approachable weight and clear touch affordance.

## Signature relationships

1. **character-led motivation.** Mascot and illustration behavior make progress feel social rather than abstract.
2. **rounded physicality.** Type, controls, and shapes share approachable weight and clear touch affordance.
3. **feedback as reward.** Success, streak, and correction states visibly change the learner's emotional momentum.
4. **lesson-first hierarchy.** Playfulness remains subordinate to the next learning action.

## Character-led motivation in practice

Centered progress path with offset illustration zones. Mascot and illustration behavior make progress feel social rather than abstract.

Type, controls, and shapes share approachable weight and clear touch affordance.

Success, streak, and correction states visibly change the learner's emotional momentum. Playfulness remains subordinate to the next learning action.

Reward feedback that can be reduced without hiding status.

## Adaptation rules

1. Suitable contexts: learning products that need motivation and repeated return, and consumer onboarding where feedback can build confidence.
2. Preserve: character-led motivation and rounded physicality.
3. Poor fit: high-stakes workflows where character animation could weaken seriousness or delay action.

## Failure modes

1. Adding mascot reactions without a real learning or state job.
2. Making every control equally playful and losing task priority.
3. Using reward color for errors or destructive actions.
