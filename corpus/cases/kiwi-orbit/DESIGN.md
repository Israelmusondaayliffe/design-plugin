## Adaptation rules

- Suitable contexts: international travel booking and itinerary products, and commerce systems with volatile inventory and recovery needs.
- Preserve: travel-task consistency and cross-platform booking.
- Poor fit: simple local catalogs with no itinerary or disruption state.

## Failure modes

- Showing a low fare without the conditions that define it.
- Designing only around short place names and one currency.
- Hiding disruption recovery behind generic support links.
