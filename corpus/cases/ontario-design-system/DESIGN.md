# Ontario Design System

## Visual thesis

Content and controls organize around completing a public service rather than promoting the agency. Layout and component behavior allow text expansion and language change.

## Signature relationships

**government task orientation**

Content and controls organize around completing a public service rather than promoting the agency.

**bilingual-ready structure**

**accessible form grammar**

Labels, help, validation, and status remain explicit through the full task.

**official but plain tone**

## Adaptation rules

Suitable contexts: provincial or regional government services, and bilingual public forms and information tasks.

Preserve: government task orientation and bilingual-ready structure.

Poor fit: luxury marketing experiences where procedural clarity is not the main outcome.

## Failure modes

**Stop 1.** Designing around one language length.

**Stop 2.** Using official tone as permission for bureaucratic copy.

**Stop 3.** Moving validation away from the field and summary context.
