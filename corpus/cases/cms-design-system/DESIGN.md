## Adaptation rules

1. Suitable contexts: health enrollment and coverage comparison, and multi-program public services sharing a component foundation.
2. Preserve: health-program precision and multi-brand service reuse.
3. Poor fit: small editorial sites with no account, eligibility, or comparison state.

## Failure modes

1. Separating calculated results from the inputs that produced them.
2. Using program branding to change component meaning.
3. Compressing plan comparison until key differences disappear.
