Near-static presentation with direct state feedback.

## Adaptation rules

- Suitable contexts: news and cultural publishing with real editorial hierarchy, and content-heavy products that need section identity and rapid scanning.
- Preserve: editorial priority and serif-sans division.
- Poor fit: minimal product landing pages where newsroom density would add false complexity.

## Failure modes

- Copying newspaper styling without editorial prioritization.
- Using section colors as decorative gradients.
- Letting dense lists lose headline, timestamp, and byline relationships.
