## Benefit-task guidance in practice

Guided application column with persistent progress context. Complex eligibility and application work is broken into understandable decisions.

Copy, validation, and support recognize the consequences of mistakes and uncertainty.

Long forms preserve location, completion state, and routes back to work. Interaction patterns address assistive technology and broad device conditions.

Direct save and validation confirmation.

## Adaptation rules

- Suitable contexts: benefits, eligibility, and long public applications, and services where users may need to pause and resume.
- Preserve: benefit-task guidance and sensitive-context respect.
- Poor fit: short impulse-purchase flows with no need for save or evidence handling.

## Failure modes

- Showing progress that does not match saved state.
- Using generic error text for eligibility consequences.
- Requiring users to re-enter verified information.
