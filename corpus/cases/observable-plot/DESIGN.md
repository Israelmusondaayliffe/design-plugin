## Adaptation rules

- Suitable contexts: exploratory data products and analytical publishing, and teams needing reproducible chart specifications.
- Preserve: declarative visual grammar and sensible analytical defaults.
- Poor fit: interfaces where decorative charts would substitute for actual analysis.

## Failure modes

- Choosing a mark because it is visually novel rather than suited to the question.
- Layering annotations until data becomes unreadable.
- Treating defaults as proof that scales and accessibility are correct.
