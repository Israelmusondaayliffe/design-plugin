2. **themeable semantics.** Tokens preserve roles across products and branded contexts.
3. **accessible component contracts.** Keyboard, focus, names, and status behavior are part of component use.

## Adaptation rules

1. Suitable contexts: communications platforms with technical configuration, and multi-product SaaS needing themeable but stable behavior.
2. Preserve: communication clarity and themeable semantics.
3. Poor fit: single-purpose brand pages with little application state.

## Failure modes

1. Changing token meanings while applying a new theme.
2. Hiding configuration errors in generic banners.
3. Treating documentation examples as finished product copy.

## Evidence confidence and gaps
