# SAP Fiori

## Visual thesis

Pages foreground the work and information relevant to a user's business role. The same semantics survive compact, cozy, desktop, and mobile contexts.

## Signature relationships

| Relationship | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| role-based focus | Pages foreground the work and information relevant to a user's business role. |
| adaptive enterprise density | The same semantics survive compact, cozy, desktop, and mobile contexts. |
| object-page structure | Complex records expose overview, detail, action, and related information in layers. |
| transaction continuity | Long business processes preserve state, validation, and recovery. |

## Role-based focus in practice

Shell and object hierarchy shifting by density mode. Pages foreground the work and information relevant to a user's business role.

The same semantics survive compact, cozy, desktop, and mobile contexts.

Complex records expose overview, detail, action, and related information in layers. Long business processes preserve state, validation, and recovery.

Short continuity cues for navigation and transactions.

## Adaptation rules

- Suitable contexts: large enterprise applications with role-specific workflows, and systems spanning desktop and mobile business tasks.
- Preserve: role-based focus and adaptive enterprise density.
- Poor fit: small editorial experiences where enterprise object structure would burden the content.

## Failure modes

- Showing every available field instead of role-relevant information.
- Changing action semantics between density modes.
- Breaking long transactions when the viewport or device changes.
