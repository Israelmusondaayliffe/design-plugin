## Outdoor context in practice

Media-rich browse grid leading to structured product specification. Product and editorial imagery connect equipment to real environments and uses.

Fit, size, technical attributes, stock, and fulfillment remain easy to compare.

Guidance and storytelling support product choice without burying purchase actions. Brand color and texture support the outdoor subject rather than simulate nature decoratively.

Quiet variant and inventory feedback.

## Adaptation rules

1. Suitable contexts: outdoor and equipment commerce, and retailers joining educational content with technical product comparison.
2. Preserve: outdoor context and specification clarity.
3. Poor fit: pure digital subscriptions with no meaningful product media or physical specifications.

## Failure modes

1. Using scenic photography without showing product evidence.
2. Hiding fit or fulfillment details below brand storytelling.
3. Turning natural color into low-contrast earth-tone decoration.
