## Adaptation rules

Suitable contexts: local-service marketplaces, and matching products that need qualification before price or availability.

Preserve: service-match clarity and human proof.

Poor fit: instant commodity checkout where a long match flow would add friction.

## Failure modes

**Stop 1.** Collecting personal details before showing value.

**Stop 2.** Using reviews without enough job context.

**Stop 3.** Losing request state between customer and provider views.
