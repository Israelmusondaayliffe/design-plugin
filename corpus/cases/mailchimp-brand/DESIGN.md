## Editorial wit in practice

Asymmetric story blocks with generous reading intervals. Unexpected composition and concise language give a familiar marketing tool a distinct voice.

Drawn media and typography share the narrative rather than decorate finished copy.

A forceful brand pair creates identity while supporting colors stay secondary. Handmade edge and type choices add character inside a controlled layout.

Small characterful reveals that never gate content.

## Adaptation rules

- Suitable contexts: creative marketing products with owned illustration and a confident voice, and editorial campaigns that can support irregular composition.
- Preserve: editorial wit and illustration partnership.
- Poor fit: regulated task interfaces where expressive irregularity could obscure state or instructions.

## Failure modes

- Using yellow across all backgrounds until calls to action disappear.
- Adding quirky illustration with no relation to the message.
- Applying handmade type treatments to dense controls or data.
