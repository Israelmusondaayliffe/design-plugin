## Adaptation rules

Suitable contexts: HR, finance, and workforce applications, and enterprise products requiring inclusive reusable components.

Preserve: people-centered enterprise work and systematic components.

Poor fit: media-led discovery pages with little structured work.

## Failure modes

**Stop 1.** Reducing people to dense rows without context.

**Stop 2.** Forking component behavior for one department.

**Stop 3.** Showing every field before the user needs it.
