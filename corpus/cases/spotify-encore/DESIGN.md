## Adaptation rules

1. Suitable contexts: media ecosystems spanning many devices and audience modes, and multi-brand products that need consistent controls around highly variable content.
2. Preserve: multi-platform rhythm and content-first color.
3. Poor fit: static editorial pages with no recurring media or playback behavior.

## Failure modes

1. Letting album art colors overwrite action and status semantics.
2. Using motion as atmosphere during urgent playback controls.
3. Forcing one visual theme across every platform context.
