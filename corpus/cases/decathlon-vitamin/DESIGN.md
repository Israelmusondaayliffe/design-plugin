## Adaptation rules

1. Suitable contexts: large multi-category retail platforms, and commerce systems with technical products and varied customer expertise.
2. Preserve: sport-family breadth and performance hierarchy.
3. Poor fit: boutique editorial commerce where a single category owns the full visual world.

## Failure modes

1. Giving every sport a separate control language.
2. Burying technical suitability below promotional imagery.
3. Using brand blue for stock, selection, and navigation simultaneously.
