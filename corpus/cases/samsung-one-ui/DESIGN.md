# Samsung One UI

## Visual thesis

Information and controls account for large screens and one-handed use. Upper regions orient while lower regions concentrate frequent interaction.

## Signature relationships

1. **reach-aware composition.** Information and controls account for large screens and one-handed use.
2. **view-action separation.** Upper regions orient while lower regions concentrate frequent interaction.
3. **platform continuity.** Settings, navigation, and feedback reuse familiar Samsung behavior.
4. **large-screen adaptation.** Layouts expand without merely stretching a phone column.

## Reach-aware composition in practice

Vertically separated viewing and action zones. Information and controls account for large screens and one-handed use.

Upper regions orient while lower regions concentrate frequent interaction.

Settings, navigation, and feedback reuse familiar Samsung behavior. Layouts expand without merely stretching a phone column.

Platform-consistent feedback with reduced-motion support.

## Adaptation rules

- Suitable contexts: Android applications for Samsung devices, and mobile products needing one-handed and large-screen behavior.
- Preserve: reach-aware composition and view-action separation.
- Poor fit: dense desktop tools whose main interaction depends on pointer precision.

## Failure modes

- Placing frequent actions outside comfortable reach.
- Scaling phone layouts without rethinking large screens.
- Inventing gestures that conflict with platform behavior.
