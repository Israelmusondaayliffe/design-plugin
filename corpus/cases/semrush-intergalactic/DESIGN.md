## Adaptation rules

- Suitable contexts: marketing analytics and recommendation products, and dense SaaS where data must lead directly to action.
- Preserve: marketing-data scan and expressive data accents.
- Poor fit: simple storytelling pages with no need for filters or repeated metrics.

## Failure modes

- Coloring every metric and destroying comparison.
- Showing recommendations without the evidence that produced them.
- Separating filters from the chart or table they change.
