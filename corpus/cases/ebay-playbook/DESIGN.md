## Visual thesis

Price, condition, seller, delivery, and trust information remain separable in repeated listings. Shared patterns support two roles with different decisions and responsibilities.

## Adaptation rules

- Suitable contexts: large marketplaces with variable inventory, and commerce products serving both buyers and sellers.
- Preserve: marketplace scan and buyer-seller symmetry.
- Poor fit: single-product luxury storytelling where marketplace density would weaken focus.

## Failure modes

- Hiding condition or seller context behind visual polish.
- Letting inconsistent imagery break scan order.
- Using promotional color for transaction status.
