# California Design System

## Visual thesis

Common components and content patterns reduce variation across agencies. Tasks remain usable on small screens and constrained connections.

## Signature relationships

1. **statewide service consistency.** Common components and content patterns reduce variation across agencies.
2. **mobile public access.** Tasks remain usable on small screens and constrained connections.
3. **plain information hierarchy.** Headings, summaries, lists, and actions support quick public-service scanning.
4. **accessible defaults.** Semantics, focus, contrast, and form guidance are part of the baseline.

## Statewide service consistency in practice

Single public-task spine with responsive agency context. Common components and content patterns reduce variation across agencies.

Tasks remain usable on small screens and constrained connections.

Headings, summaries, lists, and actions support quick public-service scanning. Semantics, focus, contrast, and form guidance are part of the baseline.

Minimal direct feedback suited to constrained devices.

## Adaptation rules

Suitable contexts: government portals spanning many agencies, and mobile-first public information and service tasks.

Preserve: statewide service consistency and mobile public access.

Poor fit: highly branded entertainment products with no cross-agency consistency need.

## Failure modes

**Stop 1.** Letting agencies fork behavior while keeping only shared colors.

**Stop 2.** Building desktop-first forms that require horizontal scanning.

**Stop 3.** Using generic headings that do not preview task outcomes.

## Evidence confidence and gaps
