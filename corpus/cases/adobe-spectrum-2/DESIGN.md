# Adobe Spectrum 2

## Visual thesis

Spectrum 2 demonstrates that a professional system can become more expressive without becoming less disciplined. Brighter color, friendlier geometry, icon changes, depth, and refreshed type are useful only because they sit inside a deliberate attention hierarchy and accessibility model.

## Signature relationships

- **Expression plus hierarchy.** Color and shape are allowed to be visible, but they have jobs.
- **Approachable icon language.** Rounded, thicker iconography shifts tone while staying systematic.
- **Navigation versus content.** Major structural regions need clear separation before decorative surfaces are added.
- **Depth for focus.** Layering should clarify relationships and transient UI.
- **Accessibility constrains expression.** Stronger visual personality still has to work across contrast and platform needs.

## Layout and density

Creative tools often need dense working areas plus visually quieter navigation and chrome. Use depth and region separation to distinguish workspace, tools, panels, and transient overlays. Do not give every panel equal visual weight.

## Typography

Use type to distinguish working labels, content, and expressive moments. A refreshed brand face can coexist with compact product text, but operational controls should remain highly legible.

## Color and surfaces

Allow more chromatic range than a neutral enterprise system, but maintain explicit attention order. Strong accents should identify actions, selection, status, or creative content rather than become ambient decoration everywhere.

## Components and interaction

Icons, controls, panels, menus, and overlays should share one visual language. Rounded iconography and depth should not be mixed randomly with thin unrelated symbols or arbitrary shadows.

## Adaptation rules

- Preserve attention hierarchy, coherent icon language, depth roles, and accessibility.
- Adapt expressive color and curvature to the product's own identity instead of copying Adobe branding.
- In dense creator tools, keep the working canvas more important than surrounding chrome.

## Failure modes

- Treating bright color as permission for multiple competing accents.
- Adding rounded icons without matching stroke, weight, and control geometry.
- Using elevation on every panel until hierarchy disappears.
- Copying Adobe-like visual details while ignoring the content and workspace structure.

## Evidence boundary

This is original analysis of public Adobe Spectrum and Adobe design articles. It stores no Adobe screenshots, icons, fonts, or product assets.
