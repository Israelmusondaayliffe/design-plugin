## Adaptation rules

1. Suitable contexts: collaborative planning and project platforms, and configurable work products with recurring board and item views.
2. Preserve: color-coded coordination and board-to-detail continuity.
3. Poor fit: formal reporting products that require subdued immutable presentation.

## Failure modes

1. Using color labels without text or stable semantics.
2. Separating item detail from board context.
3. Adding bright accents to every control and losing priority.
