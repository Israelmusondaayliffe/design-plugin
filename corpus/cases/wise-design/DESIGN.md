# Wise Design

## Visual thesis

Large literal messages make cost and movement of money understandable. A vivid brand field is balanced by blunt dark type and quiet transaction surfaces.

## Signature relationships

- **direct financial voice.** Large literal messages make cost and movement of money understandable.
- **high-energy brand contrast.** A vivid brand field is balanced by blunt dark type and quiet transaction surfaces.
- **proof near action.** Rates, fees, timing, and product UI appear beside the decision they support.
- **global adaptability.** Layouts and wording anticipate currencies, countries, and language variation.

## Adaptation rules

- Suitable contexts: international financial products that must explain value before conversion, and brands that can pair a strong public voice with exact transaction detail.
- Preserve: direct financial voice and high-energy brand contrast.
- Poor fit: anonymous white-label utilities whose identity must remain invisible.

## Failure modes

- Using the brand color as a fill for every transactional surface.
- Writing playful copy where legal or fee detail must be exact.
- Showing lifestyle imagery instead of rates, timing, and transfer state.
