## Adaptation rules

Suitable contexts: financial tools for non-specialists, and small-business platforms joining acquisition pages to complex product work.

Preserve: small-business reassurance and brand-to-product bridge.

Poor fit: professional trading systems whose users expect maximal density and domain shorthand.

## Failure modes

**Stop 1.** Using friendly copy to soften a material financial warning.

**Stop 2.** Carrying marketing illustration into dense reconciliation screens.

**Stop 3.** Hiding totals or status behind rounded decorative containers.
