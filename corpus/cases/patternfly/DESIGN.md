# PatternFly

## Visual thesis

Navigation and page regions orient users inside long-running administrative work. Health, warning, progress, and action states remain distinguishable at scale.

## Signature relationships

- **operations shell.** Navigation and page regions orient users inside long-running administrative work.
- **status-rich components.** Health, warning, progress, and action states remain distinguishable at scale.
- **pattern-level guidance.** Components are joined into workflows instead of presented as isolated widgets.
- **responsive complexity.** Dense controls reorganize without erasing the user's operational context.

## Operations shell in practice

Persistent shell supporting responsive work regions. Navigation and page regions orient users inside long-running administrative work.

Health, warning, progress, and action states remain distinguishable at scale.

Components are joined into workflows instead of presented as isolated widgets. Dense controls reorganize without erasing the user's operational context.

Progress and status continuity only.

## Adaptation rules

- Suitable contexts: infrastructure consoles and administrative platforms, and enterprise systems with long-running operations and many statuses.
- Preserve: operations shell and status-rich components.
- Poor fit: consumer discovery products whose main value comes from media or emotional storytelling.

## Failure modes

- Turning every status into a colored badge with no hierarchy.
- Collapsing responsive navigation without preserving location.
- Building from components while ignoring the documented workflow pattern.
