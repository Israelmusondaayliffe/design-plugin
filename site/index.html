02 / Method

How to read and use the library

Evidence is a boundary, not a badge.

The library records what a public source supports, what the corpus infers, what it recommends, and what it cannot establish. A public package does not certify an adaptation or guarantee an outcome.

Truth classes

Keep each claim’s evidence status visible.

Observed
Directly supported by a cited public source.
Inferred
Corpus analysis of source patterns, not the source owner’s stated intent.
Recommended
Adaptation guidance, not a source claim or outcome guarantee.
Unknown
Not established by the inspected public material.

Originality boundary

Borrow relationships, not identity.

Use the documented hierarchy, density, state logic, or component relationship as a starting point. Do not copy source geometry, branded colors, fonts, logos, screenshots, or decorative assets.

What stays private

Only reviewed public fields belong here.

Downloads exclude source notes, reviewer identities, internal paths, archive details, operational values, non-public cases, and third-party assets. Evidence IDs are scoped to each case.
