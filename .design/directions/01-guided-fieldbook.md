# Direction 1: Guided Fieldbook

## Decision layer

### Thesis

Lead with a clear case story, then reveal the evidence and downloads needed to adapt it responsibly.

### Why it fits

This direction treats understanding as part of the download product. A reader first learns the case's context, intent, value, quality, and limits. Evidence and file actions follow in the same visible sequence.

It is the strongest option when the Site should feel like a field guide that happens to contain downloadable data, not a file catalog with explanations added later.

### Signature traits

- A case thesis that orients the reader before controls.
- Visible transitions from explanation to evidence to download.
- Productive and expressive density on the same page.
- Editorial hierarchy without newspaper styling.

### Primary foundation

IBM Carbon owns the grid, semantic layers, action discipline, and productive versus expressive relationship.

### Adaptation axis

Reader-led case understanding.

### Supporting references

- Guardian Source contributes long-form content hierarchy only.
- GitHub Primer contributes mobile region reflow only.

### Clear risk

The narrative opening may slow a repeat visitor who already knows the case and wants a file immediately.

## Expert layer

### Composition and hierarchy

The opening grid carries the case thesis and most important context. The page then moves through intent, value, evidence, limitations, and download regions. Original abstract previews can help recognition, but the sequence remains complete without them.

### Typography and density

Expressive type is reserved for the case thesis and major reading transitions. Productive type carries metadata, evidence labels, provenance, and controls. Reading passages open up; technical regions stay compact.

### Color and surfaces

Original neutral layers carry structure. One action accent marks interactive intent, while a restrained lane color may identify classification. Rules, spacing, and a small number of contextual layers replace repeated cards and shadows.

### Interaction and motion

Readers can disclose evidence without losing their place. Filters and comparison remain available as supporting paths. Motion explains state changes only, and reduced motion preserves the same meaning.

### Feasibility

This fits the existing dependency-free static Site. It requires a new case hierarchy and download presentation, but no backend or new software.

### Forbidden drift

- No rounded-card field applied to every section.
- No IBM Plex, IBM blue, Guardian fonts, or newspaper identity.
- No provenance or limitations buried below download controls.
- No human-readable meaning that depends on imagery or animation.

## Evidence layer

The Carbon basis is supported by `CARBON-E01`, `CARBON-E02`, `CARBON-E03`, and `CARBON-E06`. Guardian's narrow content role is supported by `GUARDIAN-E04` and `GUARDIAN-E05`. Primer's mobile role is supported by `PRIMER-E02` and `PRIMER-E05`.

The inspected sources do not establish a transferable imagery system or exact motion values. The proposed image, type, color, spacing, and motion values are project recommendations that require definition and rendering after direction approval.
