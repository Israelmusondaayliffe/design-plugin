# Interview Answers

Record confirmed answers and corrections.

## Round 1: Reference foundation

- IBM Carbon Design System is confirmed as the single forensic reference.
- The existing Design Reference Library is the target product to expand through Benchmark 2. Its current implementation does not count retroactively as benchmark evidence.

## Round 2: Primary product job

- The expanded Library helps a designer move from one reference case toward an original adaptation for their own project.
- IBM Carbon remains the dominant forensic reference. Other designs may supply ideas only when each source has a narrow, named responsibility that helps the product meet an approved need.
- Secondary references must not dilute Carbon's essential design relationships, become co-equal foundations, or be blended into a generic average.

## Round 3: Data and delivery model

- The Site is primarily a place to understand and download design-reference data, not a saved project workspace.
- Downloaded material must explain four things rather than expose unexplained raw records:
  - Context: the owner source, product setting, audience, platform, study date, and known limitations.
  - Intent: the problem a design relationship appears to solve and the purpose of adapting it.
  - Value: what is reusable, when it helps, when to avoid it, and how it can support an original result.
  - Quality: the evidence level, confidence, provenance, review state, and unresolved unknowns. This is not a blanket certification.
- Version one does not need accounts, a backend, automatic sync, or server recovery.

## Round 4: Download audiences

- Each case must support both a readable brief for people and language-model workflows, plus structured data for deterministic software processing.
- The readable brief must stand on its own. Structured data is a second representation, not a substitute for clear writing.
- Every factual claim written into the Site or its downloads must be verified against the canonical corpus and its source evidence. Inferences, estimates, recommendations, limitations, and unknowns must remain labeled.
