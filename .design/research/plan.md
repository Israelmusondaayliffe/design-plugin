# Benchmark 2 research plan

## Decision

Determine which IBM Carbon design relationships should govern a download-first Design Reference Library, which traits must be rejected as source identity, and which narrowly assigned secondary references may improve specific product needs without weakening Carbon's role.

## Questions

1. Which Carbon relationships remain essential across dense catalog browsing, readable case analysis, comparison, and downloads?
2. What states, device coverage, responsive behavior, and evidence limitations can be directly observed in current official Carbon sources?
3. How should productive and expressive hierarchy divide the catalog, case detail, methodology, and download actions?
4. Which component, interaction, color, media, and density roles transfer safely without copying IBM identity?
5. Which secondary references can solve narrow needs in search and filtering, readable analysis, accessibility, and mobile behavior?
6. What makes three Carbon-derived adaptations meaningfully different while preserving one primary foundation?
7. Which claims must remain inferred, estimated, recommended, or unknown?

## Search axes

- Product type: reference catalog, documentation, evidence library, and downloadable data product.
- Primary job: find, understand, compare, download, and adapt.
- Density: compact working surfaces versus expressive reading surfaces.
- Platform: responsive web across desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Accessibility: keyboard, focus, semantics, touch, contrast, and reduced motion.
- Content hierarchy: human-readable evidence and deterministic structured data.
- Component states: filters, tabs, dialogs, comparison, download, loading, invalid, empty, and failure.
- Originality: preserve relationships while rejecting source identity and proprietary assets.

## Source order

1. Approved project brief and current Site.
2. Canonical accepted corpus records.
3. Current official public design-system and product sources.

## Targets

- 10 screened candidates.
- 6 forensic dossiers.
- 3 Carbon-derived adaptations for user approval.

## Evidence rules

- Verify factual claims against project-local or owner-published evidence.
- Record URL, retrieval date, inspected state or viewport, evidence class, confidence, and limitation.
- Keep observed, measured, inferred, estimated, recommended, and unknown claims distinct.
- Do not copy third-party identity, assets, copy, or undocumented measurements.

## Stop conditions

- A source contradicts the approved product or originality boundary.
- Current evidence cannot support a consequential Carbon claim.
- A secondary reference needs a broad or co-equal role.
- The direction system cannot represent three adaptations from the one approved primary reference without a runtime repair.
- A useful direction requires an unapproved dependency, deployment, account, external write, or asset batch.
